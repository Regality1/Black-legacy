How we work with a state

We build it, prove it, then hand it over.

01

Sit with the desk

We start where the work happens: registries, counters, the office WhatsApp group. We map every step a citizen takes and every step staff take behind them.

02

Pilot one service

One service in one ministry, live with real users and measured against the old process before anything grows.

03

Scale across MDAs

What works becomes a shared platform that other ministries, departments and agencies plug into, instead of a new system each time.

04

Hand it over

Training, documentation and full source access, so the state owns and runs what we built. That is the legacy.
